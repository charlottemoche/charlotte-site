FAQ

Why hospitality → tech?

I miss restaurants. I love the community, the chaos, and making people happy. I think engineering is a bit of the same. It's a lot of problem solving, making things better, and occasionally there's a fire to put out :)

How do you use AI in your workflow?

Working with AI makes me more efficient. I am careful to plan, read outputs, refactor, and refine. It's useful having a friend along the way, and I have a rules file that is specific to the way I work. I try to write by hand from as well, as to not lose my skills.

Why side projects instead of resting?

I enjoy learning new technologies, and building is the best way to get there. It's kind of similar to learning a foreign language: you get there faster by immersion and speaking it with others.
